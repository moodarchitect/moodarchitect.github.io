Services

Our Services

FROM MOOD TO BUILT.

From consultation and design to construction drawings, permits, and on‑site review — we connect design thinking with construction reality.

01

Consultation

Design & Construction Consultation

We provide professional consultation from the early stages of a project, helping clients understand design possibilities, project requirements, construction considerations, budget direction, and the appropriate process before moving forward.

02

Design

Architecture & Interior Design

We create architecture and interior spaces based on the relationship between people, function, context, and mood — developing each project from concept and space planning to design development and visualization.

03

Construction Drawing

Detailed & Construction Documentation

Good design should be buildable. Our construction drawing service translates design concepts into detailed technical drawings that contractors and construction teams can clearly understand and execute on site.

04

Building Permit

Permit Drawing & Submission Coordination

We prepare drawings and supporting documentation required for building permit applications, including coordination with relevant professionals and authorities throughout the submission process.

05

Design & Construction Review

Drawing, Design & On‑site Review

With experience across both design and construction, we review drawings, design details, and on‑site work to identify discrepancies, potential construction issues, and details that may affect the original design intent.
